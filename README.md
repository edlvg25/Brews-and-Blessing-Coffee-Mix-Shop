☕ Brews & Blessings Coffee Mix Shop
### Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System

📌 About the Project

**Brews & Blessings Coffee Mix Shop** is a café business that started as a small event booth around March 2025, initially serving coffee and non-coffee drinks to office employees. Through events, promotions, and word-of-mouth, the business grew and eventually opened its first physical store at SM City San Lazaro, Sta. Cruz, Manila.

As the business continues to expand through events, wedding packages, and office and delivery customers, managing daily operations through manual records and spreadsheets has become increasingly challenging.

To address these challenges, our team proposes a **Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System** designed to digitize and streamline the shop's daily business operations.

The system focuses on three core areas:

- **Sales Tracking** – Records and monitors customer orders, transactions, and sales.
- **Inventory Tracking** – Monitors stock levels and provides low-stock and out-of-stock notifications.
- **Profit and Loss Tracking** – Helps owners monitor sales, expenses, and business profitability.

🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Replace manual paper-and-Excel-based inventory and sales recording.
- Improve the accuracy and accessibility of business records.
- Provide owners with updated sales, expense, and profit/loss information.
- Reduce customer dissatisfaction caused by unavailable products.
- Improve inventory monitoring through automatic stock updates and notifications.
- Provide role-based access for Owners/Managers and Baristas.
- Support business operations through reporting, shift checks, and data backup and restoration.

✨ Key System Features

Owner/Manager Interface
- Dashboard and business overview
- Sales and transaction monitoring
- Product and inventory management
- Product costing and expense tracking
- Sales, expense, and profit/loss reports
- Low-stock and out-of-stock notifications
- User account management
- Opening and closing shift checks
- Data backup and restoration

Barista Interface
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Order creation and management
- Product and menu viewing
- Transaction processing
- Inventory visibility and stock notifications
- Daily shift-related functions
